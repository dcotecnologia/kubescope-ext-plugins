## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added

- The **Plugins** section of KubeScope as an extension: **Installed** lists the Amazon EKS add-ons, the Azure extensions, the Google Cloud add-ons and the Helm releases of the cluster, with their chart, app and latest versions, the last update and an origin filter, and updates them in the write mode; **Catalog** browses the Helm charts of Artifact Hub by @danilogco
- A click on a header sorts the add-ons, and a second one reverses it, by what the cells mean (ages, versions) by @danilogco
